WHY THE FLOOR EXCEEDS THE DECLARED 3.5 s
  The 3.5 s sleep is INSIDE the held lock(s), and the lock is released only
  after the response has been read. A same-host gap is therefore
  3.5 s + the previous response's duration, so the measured floor is a floor
  on the send-to-send spacing and sits above the declared sleep by exactly
  the response time. The sleep-outside-the-lock form would be worse than no
  lock at all: it converts sequential sends into simultaneous ones.
