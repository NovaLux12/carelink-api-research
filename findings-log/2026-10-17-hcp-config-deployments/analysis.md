### A defect in this pass's own ledger, on the record

**51 of 61 rows carry a `t_queued` and `t_done` that contain a literal `%3N`** (seq 5–55). Python's `datetime.strftime` does not implement glibc's `%3N`, so the harness stamped milliseconds as text in the batch-2 rows. `t_sent` is unaffected: it is written by `date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%S.%3NZ` **inside** the flock, so it is a real sub-second stamp and it is the field every spacing figure above is computed from. The affected `t_queued`/`t_done` values are unusable at sub-second resolution and **no figure in this pass depends on them**; they are left in place rather than repaired, because back-filling a timestamp the process did not record is the one thing a ledger must never do (area A's `run.log` caveat).
